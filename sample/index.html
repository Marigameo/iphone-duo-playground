The crease

Open, the fold runs down the middle. Partly folded, the screen curves there and small controls are hard to tap. Apple's rule: scrolling content may cross it, controls should not.

Let text and images cross

Articles, feeds and photos read fine across the middle. Apple doesn't move scrolling content.

Don't centre a lone button

A single "Buy" in the middle of a wide screen lands on the crease. Pair it, or put it to one side.

Use an even number of columns

Two or four columns put a gutter on the centre line. Three put a card on it.

Don't reveal menus on hover only

Every pose is touch. Open on tap, and check (hover: hover) before relying on hover.

Size with dvh

Closed and turned, the page is 368px tall. 100vh is taller than what's visible.

Don't read the width once

Opening the device resizes the page without a reload. Use CSS, or a ResizeObserver.
